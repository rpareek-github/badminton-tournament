Badminton Tournament Manager

Doubles fixture planner with leaderboard, semis, finals, and tie-break logic.

Doubles + leaderboard + bracket

Run your tournament without spreadsheets.

Players can register their names, the admin can create doubles teams automatically, and the app builds a round-robin schedule where each team plays every other team once. It then tracks wins, tie-breaker playoffs, semi-finals, and the final in one place.

How it works

1

Players add their names from the home page.

2

Admin logs in and creates randomized doubles teams with Marvel-style names.

3

Each team plays each other once in the round-robin stage, which already guarantees more than two matches per team when you have 4+ teams.

4

Wins give 1 point, losses give 0. Tie groups trigger playoff matches before semi-finals and the final bracket.
